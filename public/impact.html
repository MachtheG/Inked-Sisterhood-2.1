Sisterhood, Service & Showing Up

Our social impact & community outreach initiatives

Since 2022, ISH has committed to using our sisterhood, our visibility, and our collective strength to uplift girls and women in vulnerable communities. What began as a simple idea — "show up for the girls who need to see women taking up space boldly" — has grown into a consistent, intentional social impact program that reflects who we are at our core.

1. The Girl Project — Kiserian & Kibera (2022–2026)

Our flagship initiative in collaboration with BeyondWaterOrg, supporting teenage girls in rescue centres and safe homes.

2022

Year 1 of Impact

  • First official Girl Project visits
  • Delivered basic toiletry packs and essential supplies
  • Held empowerment circles focused on confidence, self-worth, and dreaming beyond circumstance
  • Built relationships with centre leadership to understand long-term needs
2023

Year 2 of Impact

  • Returned with expanded toiletry packs and personal care items
  • School fees beneficiary in Kiserian completed high school and sat KCSE exams
  • Strengthened mentorship through one-on-one conversations and group sharing
2024

Year 3 of Impact

  • Curated dignity kits: sanitary pads, personal hygiene essentials, self-care items
  • Conducted soft-skills empowerment session on confidence, boundaries, and self-expression
  • Captured stories of hope and resilience
  • Delivered food packs to Kibera families following heavy floods
2025

Year 4 of Impact

  • Expanded love baskets with stationery packs and exam kits for candidates
  • Supported a partner rescue home with hygiene packs
  • Facilitated a "Sisterhood Circle" — a safe space for girls to speak, laugh, and connect
  • Introduced affirmation cards and journaling activities
2026

Year 5 of Impact

  • Continued annual tradition with exam kits, stationery, toiletry, and dignity essentials
  • Delivered powerful empowerment message on worth and potential
  • Documented visits for long-term storytelling and accountability
  • Strengthened commitment to making The Girl Project a permanent ISH CSR pillar

2. Wings of Hope Rescue Centre — Teen Mums Support

In partnership with VW Babes, ISH extended its impact to support vulnerable and orphaned teen mothers. 60+ teen mums supported.

2024 Visit

  • Contributed essential supplies for daily needs
  • Supported young mothers with dignity kits and baby care items
  • Shared moments of encouragement

2026 Visit

  • Additional contributions to support growing needs
  • Strengthened partnership with VW Babes
  • Engaged teen mums in conversations around confidence, healing, and rebuilding

Our Commitment Moving Forward

In 2026, as ISH celebrates 10 years since inception, our vision for social impact remains clear:

  • ✦ Continue expanding The Girl Project to reach more rescue homes
  • ✦ Strengthen partnerships with women-led community organisations
  • ✦ Build sustainable, long-term support systems for girls and young mothers
  • ✦ Use our platform, visibility, and sisterhood to inspire confidence, dignity, and hope

💞 ISH at 10: A Decade of Sisterhood, Service & Showing Up

Our impact is not measured only by the items we give, but by the presence we bring, the confidence we inspire, and the spaces we create for girls and women to feel seen, valued, and supported.
